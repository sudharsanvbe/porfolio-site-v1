01
EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Measuring recovery through movement.

Designed an IMU-based embedded system using an ESP32 to quantify recovery metrics for ACL rehabilitation patients. The device provides objective, data-driven recovery tracking as an alternative to subjective clinical assessment.

02
THE PROBLEM

From subjective assessment to data.

Traditional ACL recovery tracking relies on periodic clinical evaluations. This lacks granularity and fails to capture daily movement deviations.

03
ENGINEERING SOLUTION
01 Sensor Fusion

Accelerometer and gyroscope data used to calculate joint movement.

02 Edge Processing

ESP32 performs local filtering to reduce latency.

03 Human-Centered Design

Integrated into a breathable compression sleeve.
