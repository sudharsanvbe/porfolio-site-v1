Re-Spectable

Founder · In partnership with VHS Chennai
“Turn your Hindsight into Someone else's Foresight.”

When Krshay was five, a pair of glasses changed his reality. Diagnosed with astigmatism, he hadn't realised the world was blurry until that first monumental shift into clarity. That clarity allowed him to become a student-athlete and a professional league cricketer.

Re-Spectable is an online database, based in India, connecting high-quality used glasses with those who need them most — old frames on new faces. The platform uses lensometer verification and a QR-code ledger for clinical accuracy and offline field sorting. Active in 5 cities across India, in partnership with VHS Chennai.

1M1B AppliedAI

Curriculum Designer & Pilot Facilitator

Krshay designed a 3-tier AI curriculum for deployment in 1M1B innovation labs across Northeast India — where only 18.5% of rural schools have internet access, and 280 million workers face displacement from AI by 2030 (McKinsey, 2023).

The curriculum runs entirely in-browser, uses Indian datasets and Northeast Indian contexts, and embeds LLM prompts at every point a student or teacher might get stuck.

“Finally, a session which has helped and will be applicable in life.” — Student, Young Scholar's Academy

A two-day pilot was conducted at Young Scholar's Academy, Bangalore (June 16–17, 2026) with 55 students from Grades 8–9. Over 8 hours, students with little to no prior AI experience built image-classification models to solve real community problems.
